About BIR Industries

An integrated forge-to-finish manufacturer from India's engineering heartland.

BIR Industries is a manufacturer of hydraulic fittings and forged engineering components, located in Industrial Area C, Giaspura, Ludhiana, one of India's largest hubs for forging, fasteners and tractor components.

We control every critical step ourselves: billet cutting, closed-die forging on presses up to 1000 tonnes, CNC turning, threading, inspection and packing. Owning the whole process lets us hold tight tolerances, react quickly to schedule changes and keep pricing competitive for both domestic and international buyers.

Every part starts as a 3D model, which is why you can examine our products in 3D below. Share your drawing or sample and we will forge and machine it to your specification.

  • Ready stock of common forgings for faster lead times
  • Supplying across India, with export enquiries welcome

Manufacturing

Forge to finish, under one roof

A controlled sequence of operations, each with its own checks, so problems are caught at the step that causes them.

  1. 01

    Design & tooling

    Drawing review and 3D modelling; forging dies and machining programmes developed for each part.

  2. 02

    Material & cutting

    Steel bar verified against mill certificates, then cut to weight-controlled billets on automatic circular saws.

  3. 03

    Closed-die forging

    Billets heated and forged on 1000 T and 630 T presses for dense, continuous grain flow.

  4. 04

    CNC machining

    Bores, seats, threads and grooves turned on CNC lathes to drawing tolerances.

  5. 05

    Finishing

    Deburring, cleaning and surface treatment to the specified finish.

  6. 06

    Inspection & dispatch

    Dimensional and thread gauge checks, batch labelling, protective packing and dispatch.

Quality & standards

Made to the standard your application demands.

Hydraulic fittings fail where it costs most: under pressure, in the field. Our quality plan is built around the features that matter: material, thread, seat and bore.

Documentation available with supply

  • Material test certificates (mill TC)
  • Dimensional inspection reports
  • First-article / sample approval reports
  • Plating thickness and salt-spray results (ISO 9227) on request
QC-1

Incoming material

Steel grade and size verified against mill certificates before cutting. Heat numbers recorded for traceability.

QC-2

First-piece approval

The first forging and first machined part of every setup are fully measured before the batch runs.

QC-3

In-process checks

Periodic checks of critical dimensions with calibrated instruments and GO / NO-GO thread gauges.

QC-4

Final inspection

Visual and dimensional sampling before packing. Every batch is labelled with part number, size and quantity.
